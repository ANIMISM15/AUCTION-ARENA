How to Play

1. Guest, Gmail & Browser Accounts

Start right away as a Guest or link your Gmail / Browser Account from the top bar so your ID, friends, and leaderboard progress stay saved.

2. Equal Purses & 5-Card Cap

Every player receives an equal starting purse ($20, $50, or $100) and can buy up to 5 characters maximum.

3. Full Team = Free Remaining Slots

Once a player or side completes all 5 cards, remaining players can claim open slots for $0 (Free) and cannot pass while their squad still has empty spots.

4. Fair AI Judge & 200 Fighters

Choose Anime, Cartoon Fiction, Webseries Fiction, or Mix. The AI Judge scores teams fairly either Category-Wise or by Total Power Level.
